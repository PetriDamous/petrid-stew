# Ingredients

## Main Ingredients

- 2 pounds ground turkey **or** ground chicken
- 2 limes
- 1 to 2 bags frozen vegetables
- 1 to 1½ cups chicken broth

## Seasonings

Use whatever seasonings sound good to you.

* Have a bunch in the pantry.

---

# Tools Needed

- Large cooking pot
- Cutting board
- Knife
- Spatula
- Cooking oil spray
- Disposable gloves
- Measuring cup

---

# Freezing Instructions

Probably will not need the freezing and thawing instructions due to the expiration date of the chicken (10/16/2026 I believe), but I will leave them here anyways.

## Freezing Ground Turkey or Ground Chicken

If you are not cooking the meat right away:

1. Leave the meat in its original packaging.
2. Place it in the freezer.
3. Write the date on the package if possible.

For best quality, try to use within a few months.

---

# Thawing Instructions

## Refrigerator Method (Recommended)

1. Move frozen meat from the freezer to the refrigerator.
2. Place it on a plate or tray in case it leaks.
3. Let it thaw for about 24 hours.

This is the safest method.

## Cold Water Method

If you forgot to thaw the meat:

1. Keep the meat sealed in its original package.
2. Place it in a bowl of cold water.
3. Replace the water every 30 minutes.
4. Cook immediately after thawing.

## Do NOT

- Leave meat on the counter all day.
- Use hot water to thaw meat.

### Resources

- USDA Food Safety: https://www.fsis.usda.gov
- Safe Thawing Guide: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/big-thaw-safe-defrosting-methods

# Cooking Instructions

## Step 1: Prepare Ingredients

### Cut the Limes

1. Place limes on a cutting board.
2. Cut each lime in half.
3. Set aside.

### Gather Everything

Make sure you have:

- Meat
- Seasonings
- Chicken broth
- Frozen vegetables
- Clean spatula

---

## Step 2: Prepare the Pot

1. Place the cooking pot on the stove.
2. Spray the bottom of the pot with a light layer of cooking oil.

You only need enough spray to lightly coat the bottom.

---

## Step 3: Add the Meat

1. Put on gloves from the pantry.
2. Remove the meat from the package.
3. Place the meat into the pot.

---

## Step 4: Season the Meat

### How Much Seasoning?

There is no exact science here.

For 2 pounds of meat:

- Start with about 2 to 3 scoops per seasoning you wish to add using the biggest tablespoon tool we have on the counter of each seasoning you want to use.

Going to have to wing it and do what you think is right since there is no science be hind it.

### Add Lime Juice

1. Squeeze both limes over the meat.

### Don't Worry About Perfect Coverage

The seasoning does not need to be evenly spread.

As the meat cooks and gets broken apart, everything will mix together.

---

## Step 5: Start Cooking

1. Place the pot on the large front-left burner.
2. Turn the burner to approximately **8**.

- Use the larger burner setting.
- On this stove, that usually means turning the knob to the right.

### Watch the Pot

When the meat starts sizzling heavily or lightly smoking:

1. Lower the heat to approximately **6**.
2. Begin stirring.

---

## Step 6: Cook and Break Up the Meat

As the meat cooks:

1. Stir frequently.
2. Use the spatula to break large chunks into smaller pieces.

### For Ground Chicken

Cook until:

- Meat is white throughout.
- No pink remains.

### For Ground Turkey

Cook until:

- Meat is light brown throughout.
- No pink remains.

If you still see pink, keep cooking and stirring.

---

## Step 7: Add Chicken Broth

Once the meat is fully cooked:

1. Pour in 1–1½ cups of chicken broth.

Odds are you might just need 1 cup.  Use what is in the fridge first. Got another box in the pantry.

Once new broth is opened it will have to be put in the fridge.

### Why?

The broth helps:

- Add moisture
- Prevent burning
- Prevent drying out
- Mix flavors throughout the dish

### Don't Stress About Exact Measurements

If the mixture looks dry:

- Add a little more broth.

If it looks too watery:

- Let it cook longer.

---

## Step 8: Use a Clean Spatula

Before touching the vegetables:

- Wash the spatula that touched the raw meat

**OR**

- Use a new clean spatula

---

## Step 9: Add Frozen Vegetables

Add:

- 1 bag or 2 bags.
- Depends on what you want.
- I got two bags of the stir fry and squash. You can mix and match.

### Optional

Add a little more seasoning to the vegetable layer before mixing.

Add whatever you feel.

### Mix Everything Together

1. Add vegetables.
2. Stir thoroughly until everything is mixed.

---

## Step 10: Simmer

Keep the burner at approximately **6**.

Cook for **20–30 minutes**.

During this time:

- Stir every few minutes.
- Scrape the bottom occasionally.
- Add more chicken broth if things start looking dry.

### What It Should Look Like

By the end:

- Vegetables are hot and tender.
- Meat remains moist.
- Most liquid has cooked down.

---

# Storing Leftovers

## Refrigerator

Store leftovers in sealed containers.

Use within:

- 3–4 days

## Freezer

Store leftovers in freezer-safe containers.

### Reheating

If frozen:

1. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator.
2. Microwave until hot throughout.
3. Stir halfway through heating.

# Quick Version

1. Spray pot with cooking oil.
2. Add meat.
3. Add seasonings.
4. Squeeze 2 limes over meat.
5. Heat on 8.
6. Lower to 6 once cooking starts.
7. Stir and break meat apart.
8. Cook until no pink remains.
9. Add 1–1½ cups chicken broth.
10. Use a clean spatula.
11. Add 1–2 bags frozen vegetables.
12. Stir together.
13. Cook 20–30 minutes.
14. Stir occasionally.
15. Add broth if needed.
16. Serve and enjoy.
